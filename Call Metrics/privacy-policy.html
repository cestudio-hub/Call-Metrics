01

Introduction

GRANDBIZ LEARNING SOLUTIONS LLP ("Company", "we", "our" or "us"), operating under the brand name CallMetrics, is committed to protecting the privacy and personal information of its customers, employees, authorised users and website visitors.

CallMetrics is a business software platform providing call tracking, call analytics, employee activity monitoring, GPS-based location tracking, call recording management and reporting services.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, disclose and protect information when you visit our website, register for an account, use our mobile application or access our services.

By using CallMetrics, you acknowledge that you have read this Privacy Policy. Where consent is legally required, we will obtain the appropriate consent separately.

02

Scope of This Policy

This policy applies to:

  • Website visitors.
  • Registered customers.
  • Business account administrators.
  • Employees and agents using CallMetrics.
  • Individuals whose information is processed through customer accounts.
  • Individuals contacting our support team.
03

Information We Collect

Depending on the services enabled and permissions granted, we may collect the following information.

3.1 Account and Registration Information

  • Full name.
  • Business or organisation name.
  • Email address.
  • Mobile number.
  • Username and authentication information.
  • Account registration details.
  • Subscription and billing information.

3.2 Call and Telecommunication Information

Depending on supported device functionality and authorised integrations:

  • Incoming and outgoing call records.
  • Calling and receiving phone numbers.
  • Call date and time.
  • Call duration.
  • Answered, missed and rejected call status.
  • Available call activity information.
  • Associated employee or agent details.

3.3 Call Recording Information

Where recording functionality is available and lawfully enabled:

  • Audio recordings.
  • Recording timestamps.
  • Call duration.
  • Associated call metadata.
  • Recording access and download activity.

CallMetrics does not authorise unlawful recording of private conversations. Customers are responsible for ensuring that required notices, permissions and legal requirements are fulfilled before enabling recording functionality.

3.4 Location Information

Where location functionality is enabled and the required permissions are granted:

  • GPS coordinates.
  • Current or last reported location.
  • Location timestamps.
  • Location history, where enabled.
  • Associated employee identification.
  • Location tracking status.

Location information may be collected while the application is active or in the background, but only where the relevant functionality, device permissions and applicable legal requirements permit it.

3.5 Device and Technical Information

  • IP address.
  • Device model.
  • Operating system.
  • Application version.
  • Browser type.
  • Login timestamps.
  • Error reports.
  • Security and audit logs.

3.6 Payment Information

We may receive:

  • Payment transaction IDs.
  • Subscription details.
  • Payment status.
  • Invoice details.
  • Billing address and applicable tax information.

Payment card and banking credentials are generally processed by the relevant payment service provider. We do not intentionally collect or store complete payment card credentials unless a specifically disclosed and compliant payment arrangement requires it.

04

Purposes of Information Processing

We process information for legitimate and disclosed business purposes, including:

  • Creating and managing user accounts.
  • Providing call tracking services.
  • Displaying call history and call analytics.
  • Calculating employee-wise performance metrics.
  • Managing available call recordings.
  • Providing authorised GPS tracking.
  • Generating business reports.
  • Managing subscriptions and payments.
  • Providing technical support.
  • Preventing fraud and unauthorised access.
  • Maintaining platform security.
  • Complying with applicable legal obligations.

We will not use personal information for unrelated purposes without an appropriate legal basis and required notice or consent.

05

Role of CallMetrics and Business Customers

Depending on the information and purpose involved, GRANDBIZ LEARNING SOLUTIONS LLP may act in different capacities.

A. Independent processing

We determine the purposes and means of processing certain information relating to our own website visitors, account registrations, customer relationships, billing and platform security.

B. Processing on behalf of customers

Where business customers upload or manage employee information, call records, recordings or GPS information through CallMetrics, the customer generally determines the purpose and means of that processing.

In such circumstances, the customer may act as the Data Fiduciary and CallMetrics may act as its Data Processor, subject to applicable law and the relevant customer agreement.

Customers must ensure that they have the necessary authority, lawful basis, notices and permissions to provide such information to CallMetrics.

06

Employee Monitoring and Location Tracking

CallMetrics provides employee activity and GPS tracking features for legitimate business operations.

Business customers must:

  • Inform employees about monitoring activities.
  • Explain the categories of information collected.
  • Explain when location tracking is active.
  • Obtain permissions and consent where legally required.
  • Restrict monitoring to legitimate business purposes.
  • Avoid unauthorised or excessive surveillance.
  • Provide an appropriate contact for employee privacy requests.

CallMetrics does not intend to facilitate covert surveillance or unlawful tracking.

07

Call Recording and Audio Information

Customers using call recording functionality are responsible for:

  • Determining whether recording is legally permitted.
  • Providing required notices to callers and employees.
  • Obtaining necessary permissions or consent.
  • Restricting access to authorised users.
  • Using recordings for lawful business purposes.
  • Managing retention and deletion settings.

CallMetrics may provide recording retention and automatic deletion controls where technically supported.

08

Sharing of Information

We do not sell personal information as a business activity.

Information may be disclosed to:

  • Hosting and cloud infrastructure providers.
  • Payment processing providers.
  • Customer support and communication service providers.
  • Security and fraud prevention providers.
  • Authorised administrators of customer accounts.
  • Professional advisers.
  • Government authorities, courts or regulators where legally required.

Service providers are permitted to process information only for authorised purposes and subject to appropriate contractual safeguards.

09

Data Storage and Security

We implement appropriate technical and organisational security measures designed to protect information.

Depending on the service and infrastructure, these may include:

  • Authentication and access controls.
  • Role-based permissions.
  • Encryption during transmission.
  • Encryption at rest where implemented.
  • Secure infrastructure configuration.
  • Security monitoring.
  • Audit logs.
  • Backup and recovery procedures.
  • Access restrictions for recordings and location information.

No online service can guarantee absolute security.

10

Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the disclosed purposes, applicable contractual requirements and legal obligations.

Retention periods may differ by data category:

  • Account information: During the customer relationship and the necessary post-termination period.
  • Call records: According to customer configuration and applicable retention requirements.
  • Recordings: According to the selected retention setting and contractual arrangements.
  • GPS information: According to the enabled location history and retention configuration.
  • Billing records: As required by applicable accounting and tax laws.
  • Security logs: According to applicable security and legal retention requirements.

When information is no longer required, it will be deleted, anonymised or otherwise disposed of through appropriate procedures, subject to lawful retention requirements and backup cycles.

11

Data Security Incidents

We maintain procedures for identifying, assessing and responding to security incidents.

Where applicable, we will notify affected customers, individuals and competent authorities in accordance with applicable legal requirements.
